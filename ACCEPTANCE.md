# Eva acceptance scenarios

A change to Eva is judged by these, on the real machine, not by the unit checks.
`doctor.bat` runs 1-4 automatically; 5-14 are done by hand (15 is measured).

1. **Open by name.** "Open Notepad / Brave" finds the installed program (no path guessing). *doctor: launch, resolve.*
2. **Window control.** Minimise, restore, maximise, move, resize, close a window and be told the truth about the result. *doctor: window section.*
3. **Type and verify.** Type into a window and read it back. *doctor: type text and read it back.*
4. **Browse.** Open a page in the requested browser and read from it. *doctor: browser section.*
5. **Talk and look alive.** She speaks without clipped words, her mouth moves while she talks, she blinks, and she does not freeze. *doctor: audio questions; pose_lab.bat for the look; by eye in normal use.*
6. **One answer, said once.** "Hello", then "open Notepad", then "tell me about Mahatma Gandhi": each gets one reply, spoken and bubbled once, with no repeated line; the long answer starts speaking after its first sentence. *By ear; eva.log shows about two model calls for a one-step task.*
7. **Stop always works.** Say "stop" in the middle of the long answer and in the middle of a multi-step task: speech cuts at once and no further step runs. "What are you doing?" mid-task is answered immediately. *By ear; eva.log shows "Stop requested" and skipped steps.*
8. **Find out and say where from.** "What's the latest on the Chandrayaan mission?" gets a short spoken answer naming its source, without a browser opening. *eva.log shows exa__ or read_web_page calls; doctor: reading a web page.*
9. **Learns and undoes.** After a multi-step task, Activity shows a `save_skill` row within a minute or so (or none for a trivial task); "tidy my Downloads" then "undo that" puts the last moved file back.
10. **Repeats on schedule.** "Every day at <two minutes from now>, tell me a fun fact" runs, and `list_scheduled` shows it repeating.
11. **She acts out the task.** Reading: a page held in both hands, head down, eyes sweeping. Writing: pad on one forearm, the other hand writing. Typing/browsing: hands low and forward, fingers going. Thinking: hand to chin. Before a click she reaches toward the target with the hand on its side; minimising is a press-down, arranging a frame-and-slide; a verified result gets a nod. No dance or jump clip ever plays for desktop work. Every change of pose flows; nothing snaps. *By eye; pose_lab.bat has a button per activity and gesture; `node tools/motion_test.js` checks the maths.*
12. **Long answers go to the bubble and the chat window.** "Write me a 500-word passage on Kolkata": she says one short line, the bubble shows the start with "Show all" and "Notepad", it stays while the pointer is over it, and the chat window holds the full text (also after a restart). No Notepad window opens unless you click "Notepad". A short question is still answered aloud. *~/.eva/chat-history.jsonl holds the conversation.*
13. **She stays in front.** Open Settings, Chrome and File Explorer through her: she stays visible on top of each, and the window she opened keeps the keyboard focus. *By eye.*
14. **Asked-for gestures are the real thing.** "Do a squat", "wave", "twirl", "stretch", "peace sign" each show exactly that. *By eye.*
15. **No flicker.** eva.log's `Flicker probe:` line reads 0-1 % missing. *Automatic, 25 s after start; `flicker_test.bat` compares settings.*
16. **The ring menu.** Click Eva in the middle of the screen, on the taskbar, at the left and right edges and in a corner: a ring of options appears around her each time, fully readable over whatever window is behind, every option on screen. *By eye.*
17. **Learning waits for you.** After a multi-step task, a notice says a skill is waiting; tray > Skills to review shows exactly what she wrote; she uses it only after Approve. *By eye.*
18. **Helpers.** "Compare the three best budget laptops for students, with sources" -> she says a helper is on it, small lights circle her, she keeps answering other questions, and later reports with a file. *By eye; Activity shows "helper #n" rows.*
19. **From anywhere.** Select a paragraph in any app, press Win+Shift+E, type "summarise this": she summarises THAT text. Win+Shift+W, box an error message, Enter: she explains it. *By eye.*
20. **Everyday.** "Find my latest download", "how much space is left", "merge these two PDFs", "write an email to X about Y" (a draft opens; nothing sent), "help me focus for 25 minutes". *By eye; scorecard.bat covers the basics.*
21. **Like a person.** Ask her to close a window: she flies to its X and presses it. Leave her alone: she sits on one of your windows or the taskbar, looks around, yawns at night, dozes; come back and she waves. Drag her: she reacts; drop her on a window's top: she sits. *By eye.*
22. **Readable on anything.** Put a busy window behind her: her bubble (and what it says while she thinks), the status pill and the chat window are all opaque and easy to read; your messages are blue on the right, hers grey on the left. *By eye.*
23. **Administrator windows.** Open Registry Editor (approve the UAC prompt) and ask her to close it or click in it: she says at once that Windows does not let her act on an administrator window and that you can do that step; no guessed clicks, no "done". *eva.log shows `runs as administrator`.*
24. **Where you put her.** "Sit on the Notepad window": she walks there, sits on its title bar, and rides along when you move it; she is still there two minutes later. "Go to the right side": she stays there. While she walks, her bubble goes with her. "Jump": she leaves the ground. *By eye.*
25. **A video, safely.** Play a YouTube video (also Picture-in-Picture, also full screen) and ask her to click things on it: she walks over and clicks, no crash. "Pause it" / "play it": media keys, instantly. "Go to the last tab": the real last tab. Leave the video running: she sits beside it and watches. *No `CRASH REPORT` at the next start; `python tools/qt_callback_test.py wrapped` survives.*
26. **Moments.** "Thanks!": hands to her heart. Ask the time: a look at her watch. A slow action: hands on hips. A file moved: she carries a box. *By eye.*

27. **Keeps going until told.** On a long web page: "keep scrolling until I say stop" scrolls steadily; "faster" speeds it up; "stop" (or Win+Shift+X) ends it at once. "Scroll to the bottom" stops by itself at the end and says so. *Activity / eva.log show `start_repeating`, then how it ended.*
28. **Turn-based games.** Open any online tic-tac-toe, Connect Four or 2048 and say "play this": she reads the board, plays each move herself, waits for the opponent's, and announces won / lost / draw (2048: the best tile). She never loses tic-tac-toe. *By eye; `play_lab.bat` runs the same against local copies.*
29. **Real-time play.** A Flappy Bird clone and Chrome's dinosaur game (chrome://dino): she plays on her own, many presses a second, and reports the score when it ends; she stands clear of the game while playing. A reaction test ("press space when it turns green") reacts within a frame or two. *By eye; `play_lab.bat flappy runner`.*

30. **Settings.** Tray > Settings opens a readable window; change bubble colours and gesture speed and press Save: both change at once. Change Size: the window says it applies after a restart, and after Restart Eva it has. Say "talk less": she lowers how much she says aloud and says so. *`%USERPROFILE%\.eva\settings.json` holds only what you changed.*
31. **Instant file search.** "Find the PDF that mentions <a word inside one of your PDFs>": the right file within a second or two. *eva.log shows `Windows Search: N results in X ms`; with the Windows Search service stopped she still finds files by name (`source: eva index`).*
32. **Looking twice costs nothing.** Ask "what is on my screen?" twice without changing anything: the second answer is immediate. Change something (open a menu) and ask again: a fresh look. *eva.log shows `screen unchanged, cached` only for the unchanged screen.*
33. **Failed-task replay.** Ask for something that cannot work (click a greyed-out button): tray > Failed tasks opens a page with each step, its result and a picture after each on-screen step; no picture after a password step. *`%USERPROFILE%\.eva\replays` keeps the newest 10.*
34. **Long sessions.** Leave Eva running for a working day: tray > Status shows "Process: ... MB, ... threads" with no `<--` warning, and eva.log's `Vitals:` lines stay level. *`python tools/soak_test.py` passes.*
35. **Predictive help.** Switch on Settings > Initiative > "Offer my usual routines". Open the same program at about the same time on three days; on the fourth, around that time, she offers it once. "Yes" opens it; "no" twice mutes it for two weeks; "don't ask again" stops it. A banking or payment site or app is never offered ("what routines do you know?" never lists one). Nothing is offered while you are away, in full screen, or while she is busy.

36. **Finding text.** With a YouTube page open, "click the video called Japan's dear city" (misheard on purpose): the right title is clicked within about a second. *eva.log: `Located ... by its text`.*
37. **Finding pictures.** "Click the thumbnail with a man holding a deer": the click lands on that thumbnail, not to its upper-left. *tray > Status shows `Vision: ..., coordinates measured as norm1000` (or the measured format) a minute after start.*
38. **No 90-second hangs.** With the vision service busy, a look or locate answers within about a minute at most, saying the service is busy. *eva.log has no `did not finish within 90s` for vision tools.*
39. **Misheard words.** Say something with a homophone in a clear context ("scroll faster, it's too slow"): she acts on the meaning. Say an ambiguous name: she asks one short question with her best guess. Spell a word letter by letter: she uses the word. "Dewas top" stops her.
40. **No empty claims.** "Pause it" always produces a pause action (or an honest "I couldn't"), never "It's paused" with nothing done; no bracketed stage directions are spoken.

41. **Administrator windows, by choice.** With WizTree (or any administrator window) open, ask her to click in it: she explains she cannot and offers to restart as administrator. Say yes: Windows shows its approval prompt; approve, and after a few seconds she can click Scan. Decline: she comes back as before.

42. **Structure first, everywhere.** In your browser (Wordle): "click Play" clicks the Play button, not a "Play" link elsewhere; in a desktop app (Notepad's menu, VS Code's sidebar, Settings): "click File", "click Explorer", "click Bluetooth"; on the desktop: "open the Steam shortcut". Each is found in about a second. *eva.log: `Located ... in the window's structure: button 'Play'`.*

43. **Unblocking herself.** Open a page with a cookie or sign-in banner over it and ask her to do something behind it: after at most a few attempts she says what is in the way, deals with it (or asks), and carries on -- she never runs out of steps repeating the same click. *eva.log: `Stuck check:` followed by a different action.*
44. **One status, in her bubble.** While she works the bubble above her reads "Thinking ...", then what she is doing ("Looking for ..."); the line below her says only Listening / Muted / Paused or Speaking. Her spoken message is never replaced mid-read.

45. **Corrections while she works.** Ask for a multi-step task, then correct her mid-way ("no, press Enter first"): she does not start over; her next step follows the correction. *eva.log: `Folded into the running task:`, no `Turn interrupted` except after "stop".*
46. **No endless thinking.** However hard the question, she answers, acts or asks within about 45 s of thinking; "stop" ends even a long think at once. *eva.log: `Model call cut short:` when it happens.*

47. **Learns a game, plays fairly.** "Play Wordle" (or Minesweeper, Sudoku, 2048-like games) on a fresh PC: she looks up how to play (not the answer), saves a rules card, then plays move by move -- each guess entered and confirmed as the rules say, each result read from the board and noted. Ask her mid-game what she knows so far: she answers from her notebook. She never looks up today's answer unless you tell her to. *`%USERPROFILE%\.eva\games\wordle.json` holds the card; eva.log shows `read_screen_elements` and `game_note` each move.*

Rule: if a scenario fails on the doctor's report, fix that before adding anything.
48. **Never restarts, deletes or uninstalls without asking.** Open Windows Update while a restart is pending (or any app's Delete / Uninstall / Sign out button) and ask her to press it: an approval dialog names the real button ("Restart now") and why; refusing leaves everything as it was. Ordinary buttons (Check for updates, Restart later) do not ask. *eva.log: `not allowed (denied)` after a refusal.*
49. **She speaks.** Her greeting and replies are heard; if the online voice is unreachable she speaks with the Windows voice instead. *eva.log: no `Speech synthesis failed`; `Online voice failed ... using the Windows voice` only when it is down.*
50. **Types once.** "Open Notepad and type: hello there" -- the line appears exactly once and she says it is done. *eva.log: `type_text -> {'ok': True, ... 'verified': True`.*
51. **Stop is instant.** Ask for a long piece of writing, then say or type "stop" within a few seconds: she stops within about a second. *eva.log: `Stop requested` and `Model call cut short: stop requested` in the same second.*
52. **She feels present.** Move the mouse: her eyes follow it; hover over her: she looks at you. Speak: she nods and smiles. Leave her alone for 10+ minutes: she fidgets, yawns, stretches; after about 20 she may run about the bottom of the screen. *eva.log: `Idle life (bored N): ...`.*
53. **She looks after the PC and asks first.** When bored she may offer one fix ("Your Downloads folder has ... want me to sort out what's there?"). No: she does not ask again for a week. Yes: she looks and proposes; nothing is moved or deleted without a second yes. *eva.log: `Patrol found: ...`.*
54. **She works in your own browser.** With the extension loaded: "what tabs do I have open?" lists them; "read this page" summarises it; she fills a form field; she never types a password; a "Delete"/"Place order" button asks first. *eva.log: `Browser bridge listening`, `Browser bridge: ... connected`.*
55. **Every task has a card.** Ask her in the chat window to do something on the PC ("create a text file on the Desktop called test.txt"): a card appears and grows step by step, with Stop while she works; it ends Done / Didn't finish / Stopped. If the task changed files, Undo puts them back; Run again repeats it; a failed task has a Steps page with pictures. The cards are still there after a restart. *eva.log: `Task card: undo ... Undone`.*
56. **A welcome on the first run.** Delete `%USERPROFILE%\.eva\welcome.json` and restart her: she says "Let me show you around" and a short tour opens (key page only if no key is set). Change the voice: she speaks in it. Press Test on the microphone page and say "Hello Eva": it shows what she heard. The privacy page lists what leaves the PC. "Try me" runs a first task with the chat window open. Skip or Finish: it does not come back (tray > Welcome tour opens it again).
57. **A job of many steps.** "On makemytrip.com, search one-way flights from Kolkata to Ho Chi Minh City on 25 December for 2 adults; do not log in or book." She opens the site, fills From/To from the suggestion lists, sets the date and travellers, searches and says what the cheapest options are -- or says exactly where she got stuck. She never books, logs in or types an OTP; when only you can act she asks and waits. Say "stop" at any time. *eva.log: `Operator step N: ...`, `Operator finished` or `Operator stopped ...: <why>`.*
58. **She hears the whole sentence.** Say, starting softly: "Open Notepad and type: one two three". The first word is not lost. Say "I think..." then, after a breath, "...open the calculator": one request, not two. *eva.log: `Heard: '<the whole sentence>'`.*

59. Tool groups: the core tools plus groups she opens with use_tools; about 21k characters per call instead of 59k.
    A success claim after on-screen steps waits for one look at the result. One request budget per provider.
    The benchmark records false "done" reports. Main model: nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl-120b-a12b (the old one is
    no longer served). Benchmark on the PC 2026-10-28: 16/16, 0 false "done".
60. Hearing with NVIDIA canary-1b (Google as fallback): on the 14 kept phrases it kept every sentence opening
    Google dropped and heard the misheard words right. Tool groups measured (A/B: 16/16 vs 15/16, half the
    characters). After six screen steps of her own she hands the task to the operator. Live Wordle: a full game
    played end to end and honestly reported; a win is not yet achieved (the free vision model misreads boards).
