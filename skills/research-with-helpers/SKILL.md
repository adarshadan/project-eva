# Research with helpers

1. Split the job into 1-3 independent parts (one per option, topic or group of files).
2. `delegate` each part with a complete task: what to find, which sources or files, and what the
   result should be ("Write a one-page comparison of X and Y into a file: price, battery, reviews").
3. Tell the person in one sentence: "Two helpers are on it; I'll tell you when they're done."
4. When the reports arrive (as [system] notes), combine them into one answer. Long answers go in the
   reply (opened in Notepad); point to any files the helpers wrote.

Pitfalls
- Never delegate anything on screen, and never anything that changes the person's files.
- Quick questions do not need helpers; answer them directly.
